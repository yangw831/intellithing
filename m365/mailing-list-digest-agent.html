The Problem

Inbox rules remove the noise, and your awareness goes with it

1. The emails pile up Industry newsletters, vendor and partner updates, internal announcement lists, project distribution lists, GitHub notifications. Each one matters occasionally. Together they bury your inbox.
2. You create a rule Everything from the list goes to its own folder. Your inbox is quiet again.
3. You stop knowing what's happening A decision gets made, a proposal moves forward, a deadline is announced, and you find out weeks later. Opening the folder means facing hundreds of unread messages.
4. You ask Copilot to summarize it, every morning That helps, but you have to remember to ask, retype the prompt, and it doesn't know what it told you yesterday.

The Idea

The summary shows up on its own

An agent in your Microsoft 365 tenant that:

  • reads only the folders you pick
  • runs every morning on its own
  • groups yesterday's messages by topic and ranks them by importance
  • tracks topics across days, so it says what moved, not just what was said
  • learns which topics you care about over time
  • delivers one email to your inbox

Planned approach: a Microsoft 365 Copilot agent for setup, Microsoft Graph for reading the selected folders, and email delivery. That's one of the things we're asking about below.
